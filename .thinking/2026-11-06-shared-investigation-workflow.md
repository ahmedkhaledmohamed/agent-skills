# Shared investigation workflow

## Problem Framing

Collaborators need to discover ongoing investigations and share selected findings without creating competing copies of existing evidence. The workflow must work across authorized environments while preserving attribution and uncertainty.

## Approach

Add a portable investigation skill, an offline reference checker and independently fictional examples and behavioral cases. Keep live evidence, account configuration and local source bindings outside the reusable collection.

## Key Decisions

Reference established artifacts at their maintained sources and allow newly produced supporting artifacts to have an explicit home. Preserve inspected source revisions per contribution, require coordinator reconciliation and permit a correct no-change result.

## Outcome

Local checker tests and portability validation pass. Behavioral cases are defined, but independent agent execution and team usefulness remain unverified; publication requires separate review.
