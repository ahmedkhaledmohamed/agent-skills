# Portable workflow publication preparation

## Problem Framing

The portable collection needed an explicit reuse license and a bounded disclosure review before any public-visibility decision. Publication preparation had to remain separate from merging pending changes or changing repository settings.

## Approach

Reviewed fetched branches, pull-request history and available repository surfaces in an isolated audit copy. Prepared an MIT license proposal, clarified the public description and documented ownership and final-decision requirements without publishing private audit details.

## Outcome

The configured source/history and remote-text scans found no disclosure-pattern matches, and structural and example checks passed. Linked-project access and owner confirmation of licensing rights remain unresolved; visibility is unchanged. The contribution extractor found no matching sessions, so this record summarizes the visible task.
