# Portable specification system update

## Problem Framing

The prior workflow described source-backed requirements and change review but did not clearly distinguish feature coverage from source-file coverage or historical delivery from approved intent. A portable method also needed an executable illustration of record links and declared-scope validation.

## Approach

The update extends the existing coordinator skill and reference model with feature homes, delivery history, source-authority assessment, verification stages, and two change loops. A generic local bundle and read-only validator illustrate structural checks without requiring a specific platform or connector.

## Key Decisions

The schema remains illustrative and backward-compatible with existing record kinds. Deterministic validation checks identifiers, references, and the explicitly declared denominator; evidence truth, ownership, rollout, and requirement approval remain reviewer decisions.

## Outcome

The portable package now describes how to establish and maintain a traceable specification system, answer questions, and classify candidate gaps while preserving uncertainty and local publication authority.
