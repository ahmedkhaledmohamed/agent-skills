# Connected product writing

## Problem Framing

Product prose can state accurate facts while leaving readers to infer how they relate to the user problem or proposed change. The reusable writing guidance needed a clearer way to connect those statements without imposing one narrative structure on every artifact.

## Approach

Extend the writing and product-brief workflows with concrete term definitions, explicit logical connections and a flexible editorial checklist. Use an independently fictional example and preserve existing evidence, vocabulary and publication boundaries.

## Key Decisions

Keep paragraph-level review separate from a mandatory document template. Distinguish current capabilities, proposals and observed outcomes, and avoid treating configuration counts as evidence of effectiveness.

## Outcome

Prepared focused skill changes for review and export into the framework's maintained bundle. Offline validation checks structure and portability; it does not establish writing quality through an observed user study.
