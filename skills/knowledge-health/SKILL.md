# Inspect a knowledge base
Resolve the corpus and environment. Inspect diagnostics and their side effects before executing them.
For a read-only health request, use only confirmed read-only observability and status checks.
Report source dates, index state, errors, and unavailable checks. Distinguish corpus coverage from live service health.
Suggest targeted repair for diagnosed issues. A health request does not authorize synchronization, index rebuilds, embeddings, account changes, or external contributions.
For requested repairs, confirm targets and authority. Use approved organization connections for organization-controlled sources, never cached personal credentials.
Do not print credentials or source environment files merely to diagnose a missing integration.
