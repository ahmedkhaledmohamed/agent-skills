# Research across a knowledge base
Resolve the corpus and guidance. Decompose the question into relevant entities, periods, systems, or claims.
Use available search and graph capabilities after inspecting their contracts. Read original documents, provenance, dates, and supersession information.
Copies of one source are not independent corroboration. A large result limit does not prove exhaustive coverage.
Record scope, queries, included sources, exclusions, and missing access. Source each material claim and measurement.
Distinguish evidence, inference, contradiction, and unresolved questions. If quantitative evidence is absent, state the gap instead of substituting a vague count.
Use current authorized sources for live status. Do not synchronize, rebuild, publish, or authenticate accounts as a research side effect.
